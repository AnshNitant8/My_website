Product Case Studies

Featured Work

Real problems, real products — each case study documents how I approached discovery, defined the solution, and measured the outcome.

Case Study 01
IRCTC Tatkal — Quick Resume Booking
During Tatkal booking windows, a network error or accidental logout forces users to restart the entire booking flow — only to find seats unavailable seconds later. This is a painful, well-documented friction point with measurable drop-off.
Consumer Product
My Role
Led end-to-end product case study: user research, journey analysis, problem framing, PRD development, and prioritization (P0 feature definition). Conducted primary and secondary research on booking failure patterns.
Solution Designed
Quick Resume Booking — a session-state recovery mechanism that preserves train selection, passenger details, and seat preferences for a configurable window post-logout. Prioritized passenger data persistence and one-tap re-entry as P0 features.
Expected Impact
↓ Drop-off
Reduced abandonment during error-triggered logouts in peak Tatkal windows. Improved booking completion rate and user trust metrics.
Case Study 02
Company Profile Retrieval System
Sales teams manually searched for company data — registration details, employee strength, turnover figures — before every corporate insurance quote. The process took 20–40 minutes per request and introduced inconsistencies in proposal quality.
0-to-1 Product
My Role
Identified the operational problem through stakeholder interviews with the sales and operations teams. Owned product definition, API integration strategy (GST/MCA data sources), and end-to-end delivery.
Solution Built
An automated company profile retrieval system — integrated with GST and company registration data APIs. Single search input returns verified company data within seconds. GST-based authentication layer added to filter unqualified leads.
Measured Outcome
95%
Reduction in manual data processing time. 40% reduction in junk leads entering the sales pipeline.
Case Study 03
Master Policy Portal — 100% Paperless Issuance
Group corporate insurance policy issuance required manual document handling, offline approvals, and multi-step coordination across Sales, Operations, and Compliance. Turnaround times were long and error-prone.
Workflow Automation
My Role
Directed portal development as PM — gathered requirements from Sales, Operations, Compliance, and Legal stakeholders. Defined workflow states, approval logic, and document generation requirements. Managed Agile delivery with Engineering and QA.
Solution Delivered
A fully digital Master Policy Portal replacing the paper-based issuance workflow. Automated policy generation, digital approval workflows, structured data capture, and online document delivery — eliminating all manual processing steps.
Measured Outcome
100%
Paperless policy creation achieved. Post-policy issuance fully automated — strategic OKR delivered. Turnaround time reduced by 75%.
